# Lesson: Boost Viewer Engagement
### TriForge Media Creator Onboarding — Module 6

Engagement is the single biggest lever for growth on TikTok LIVE — it drives For You feed placement, follower conversion, and revenue. This module is the full engagement toolkit: chat management, widgets, co-streaming with other creators, replay/highlight tools, and the interaction habits that turn casual viewers into a real community.

---

## 1. The Fundamentals of Viewer Interaction

**How to actually interact with viewers:**
- **Greet them** — a personalized welcome when someone joins makes them feel noticed and more likely to stick around.
- **Acknowledge them** — thank gifters by name, loudly and visibly.
- **Ask questions** — about their day, their opinion, or the game you're playing.
- **Share stories** — personal anecdotes make the stream feel intimate rather than performative.

**Go-live habits that compound over time:**
- Stream on a consistent schedule so people can build a habit around you.
- Promote that schedule in your bio and on social media.
- Set up LIVE Events — they measurably increase both viewership and Diamond rewards.
- Balance attention between regulars and newcomers so nobody feels ignored.

**Why this all matters:** the highest-value viewer actions — following, gifting, sharing, commenting — are also the signals that get a stream surfaced on the For You feed. Engagement isn't just nice, it's the growth mechanism.

**Winning over new viewers in the first few seconds:**
- Keep your layout clean — clutter is the fastest way to lose someone browsing past.
- Create specific, celebrated actions for follows and gifts so new viewers see what's rewarded.
- Anchor each stream around a clear topic and mention it explicitly when new viewers land.

**Promotion tactics that actually move the needle:**
- Post highlight clips and your upcoming schedule to your profile.
- Have moderators repeat your intro/topic in chat for anyone who just joined.
- Run themed LIVE events.
- Cross-promote on other social platforms.

**The conversion funnel, in order:** capture attention → give a clear call to action to follow → retain interest with genuinely engaging content → convert that attention into gifts, shares, and Fan Club joins.

**Building an actual community, not just an audience:**
- Ask viewers to share the stream, run "vibe checks," and use consistent soundbites to welcome new faces.
- Recognize top viewers personally — remember real details about their lives.
- Choose moderators who build community, not just police chat — they should reinforce key messages and drive engagement themselves.
- Stay active outside the stream: post consistently, reply to comments, consider a Discord for LIVE notifications and off-platform engagement.
- Run community events — game nights, happy hours, tournaments — and promote them ahead of time.

## 2. Using LIVE Chat to Actually Know Your Viewers

LIVE chat shows every viewer comment, GIF, and emoji in real time — and clicking a username surfaces more detail about that viewer, which is your best tool for personalizing interaction.

**Chat settings worth configuring:**
- **Filter inappropriate words** — block specific words/phrases under Chat settings.
- **Control comments and Gifts** — toggle "Allow comments" and "Allow Gifts" per your preference.
- **Appoint moderators** — click a viewer's profile picture during the stream to add them as a moderator or mute them.

**In-game overlay — chat without leaving your game:**
- Click the chat icon to turn chat into a transparent floating window, then drag it to the side of your game screen.
- It shows real-time stats (LIVE status, duration, viewer count, comment count), plus comments, GIFs, follows, shares, likes, and new-viewer notifications — filterable if you want to hide specific info.

**When the overlay won't display over a full-screen game:**
- Switch the game to windowed fullscreen or windowed mode.
- Use a second monitor for the overlay.
- Use LIVE Studio's Share feature to join your own channel as a viewer from another device (via QR code or link) so you can interact from there instead.

**Making chat actually build engagement:**
- Greet new viewers by name.
- Actually respond to comments and questions.
- Thank GIF senders and new followers specifically.
- Use questions, polls, and prompts to keep it lively.
- Keep moderators focused on maintaining a respectful space.

## 3. Extended Chat — Managing High-Volume Chat Like a Pro

When chat is moving too fast to track, extended chat splits it into filtered, prioritized columns alongside your main LIVE chat.

**Setup:**
1. Undock your LIVE chat panel into a floating window.
2. Click the Settings icon in the floating chat window and select **Extended chat**.
3. Toggle on any preset groups you want (up to 3 active at once).
4. Build custom groups if the presets don't fit (see below).
5. Choose a layout: **Compact** or **Spread**.

**Preset groups:**
- **Interaction hints** — surfaces useful tags on higher-value viewers to help you build real connections.
- **Key viewers** — filters to your most engaged, valuable viewers automatically.
- **Gifts** — every Gift in one dedicated column, easy to track and acknowledge.
- **Joins and likes** — shows joins/likes from viewers above a certain gifter level, so you catch high-value viewers as they arrive.

**Building a custom group:**
1. Click **Add** in extended chat settings.
2. Name the group.
3. Under **Show messages only from**, set viewer criteria (gifter level, followers, current-LIVE gifters, etc.) — leave blank to show everyone.
4. Under **Show messages for**, check which interaction types to include (comments, likes, Gifts).
5. Click **Add**, then activate with the toggle.

**Best practices:** on a multi-monitor setup, put the extended chat window on your secondary monitor to keep your main screen clear. On a single monitor, consider turning extended chat off entirely for visibility.

**Troubleshooting:** if a group isn't showing, confirm its toggle is on and that LIVE chat is undocked (extended chat doesn't work docked). If the floating window is blocking your screen, resize it, trim the number of active groups, or switch to the compact layout.

## 4. Instant Replay & LIVE Replay

**Instant replay** continuously records in the background so you can instantly save the last few seconds/minutes after something great happens — just hit your hotkey. By default it saves to the cloud (LIVE Center); you can also enable local saving.

**Setup** (Settings > Recordings):
- **Save locally** — off by default (cloud-only); turn on to also save to a local folder.
- **Recorded source** — Game capture (auto-detected, largest visible area) or Camera (same logic). This setting only affects local saves — LIVE Center instant replays always capture your full canvas.
- **Instant replay length** — how far back it captures when triggered (e.g., 30 seconds = last 30 seconds saved).
- **Hotkey** — customizable trigger key.

**Recording gallery:** your central library for managing all recordings and instant replays, accessible via its own hotkey — this is also where you pull clips for LIVE replay.

**LIVE replay** lets you pull a saved clip from the gallery and play it back live for your viewers. Workflow: capture your best moments with instant replay first, then use LIVE replay to actually share them on stream.

**Using LIVE replay:**
- Select a video from the recording gallery to add it to your canvas and play it.
- It appears as a source on your canvas — click it to control playback via the toolbar; remove the source to end playback.
- **Floating display** — for full-screen games, enable this to trigger and monitor playback via the recording gallery hotkey without alt-tabbing out of your game. Drag the progress bar to scrub, close the window to end it.

## 5. Adding Moderators to Manage Chat

A LIVE moderator is someone you authorize — a follower or team member — to help manage chat, boost engagement, and catch violations in real time, since it's nearly impossible to track everything yourself once viewer counts climb.

**Setting a moderator:** open LIVE chat during your stream, left-click the viewer's username, click the **⋮** icon, select **Set as moderator**.

**Removing a moderator** (two paths):
- Click the settings icon on LIVE chat → **Moderator** → click **—** next to their name.
- Or: **LIVE settings** → **Moderator** → click **—**.

**Unmuting/unblocking someone:** settings icon on LIVE chat → **Mute accounts** or **Block accounts** → **Unmute**/**Unblock**.

## 6. Engagement Widgets

Widgets are overlay tools that add structure and interactivity to your scene:

- **Alert source** — real-time pop-up notifications (with animation/sound/text) for Gifts, follows, or subscriptions. Drives instant recognition, visual appeal, and more engagement since viewers see their actions get a reaction.
- **Chatbox source** — displays your LIVE chat directly on screen. Useful for highlight videos (gives context to what you were responding to) and makes viewers feel more involved when they see their name/message on screen.
- **Goal source** — displays follower or Gift goals on screen to rally viewers around a shared milestone. Clarifies what you're working toward and motivates contribution.
- **Viewer ranking source (leaderboard)** — highlights top contributors (e.g., top gifters), adding a competitive, game-like layer that tends to increase gifting overall.
- **Countdown source** — a visible timer building anticipation for an event, giveaway, or stream start, and helping you stay on schedule.

## 7. Going LIVE With Other Creators — Co-host & Multi-guest

**Co-host:** invite up to 3 other creators onto a split screen for real-time collaboration — and it unlocks LIVE matches, which boost Diamond collection. Benefits: livelier multi-host conversation, access to your co-hosts' audiences (and vice versa), and the competitive appeal of LIVE matches.

**Multi-guest:** audio-only communication with multiple guests (no camera for guests). Benefits: hearing tone and emotion makes interaction feel more natural; one-on-one time with top-contributing fans builds loyalty; you can bring in moderators to help manage the session; guests add commentary and depth to the content.

**Multi-guest specific guardrails:** both you and your guests need to follow Community Guidelines (a violation can get your LIVE suspended), and you need to be ready to shut down a guest immediately if they cross a line.

**Making the most of either feature:**
- Coordinate with co-hosts/guests ahead of time.
- Set join permissions: **Allow requests from viewers**, **Allow requests from followers only**, or **Invite only**.
- Actively engage with whoever's on with you — don't let it go flat.
- Give every host/guest a fair share of airtime.
- End sessions cleanly — click **End** and thank participants.

## 8. Gaming Co-host — Layout Deep Dive

Co-host on TikTok lets you invite up to 3 other creators into your LIVE. There are two layout modes:

- **Current scene** — best for a 2-player showdown or collab. Connect without changing any sources; viewers see both your cameras and both game screens.
- **Share screen** — best for group parties up to 4 creators. Everyone shares one big game screen, and you can switch between whose perspective is shown, with all creators able to add commentary.

**Setup (make sure LIVE Studio and TikTok are both updated first):**
1. Go live with a camera source active, then click **Co-host**.
2. Go to **Settings** and pick your layout — **Standard/Current scene** (both cameras + both game screens visible) or **Share screen** (multiple cameras + one shared big screen).
3. Invite other creators — friends, "people you may know," suggested creators, or creators currently playing the same game. Invitees have 15 seconds to accept before you'll need to resend or pick someone else.

**Accepting an invite:**
- **Current scene** — just accept and you're in.
- **Share screen** — if you have a capture source available, a pop-up lets you choose what to share before accepting. No capture source? You can still join camera-only while viewers see the other creator's shared game screen.

**Ending co-host:** click **Co-host** again, then the end button in the bottom-left of the pop-up window.

**FAQ:**
- **Virtual camera during co-host?** Yes — set it as your camera source in LIVE Studio, preview, and apply.
- **Can you join a LIVE match in both layouts?** Only "Current scene" currently supports LIVE matches.
- **Can viewers pick whose screen they see in Share screen?** No — only creators control that. Viewers wanting another creator's view need to join that creator's LIVE directly.
- **Getting audio echo?** Usually caused by running Discord or in-game voice chat simultaneously — mute one of the two channels.

## 9. Flares — Paid Exposure Boost

Flare gives your LIVE a temporary visibility boost. There are 3 tiers, representing increasing levels of impressions.

**Getting flare:** earn it by completing TikTok tasks or participating in official TikTok activities — availability shows up under **Tools**.

**Using flare:**
1. Click the flare icon.
2. Choose the tier you want to use.
3. Once active, the top-left of your screen shows total participants reached.

**Important constraints:**
- Once activated, a flare **cannot be cancelled or paused** — it runs its full course.
- Effective duration is **30 minutes**.
- If too many creators are using flares simultaneously, effectiveness drops — TikTok may prompt you to use yours at a different time.

## 10. Recording, Highlighting & Sharing Your Best Moments

**Recording your LIVE:** click record, set a storage path and format before you start (format changes don't apply retroactively to an in-progress recording). Recordings capture your local canvas only — no viewer info like Gifts or comments included, keeping quality high.

- **MP4** — default, broadly compatible.
- **MOV** — best for iOS/macOS.
- **MKV** — smaller files, higher quality; good for saving disk space or transfer costs.

**Highlights** are short clips (30 seconds–2 minutes) generated from your LIVE content, ready to view, download, crop, or post with one click — synced across LIVE Studio and mobile.

**What gets captured as a highlight:**
- **Game moments** — victories, kills, key plays.
- **Interaction moments** — Gift peaks, comment spikes.
- **Viewer join peaks** — surges in incoming viewers.
- **Manual highlights** — triggered via the Highlight button or Ctrl+1, capturing the past 2 minutes by default.
- **Manual instant marking** — flagging a moment yourself in real time.

**Why bother with highlights:** short-form clips are one of the best tools for pulling viewers into your next LIVE — they keep your content discoverable and improve conversion between sessions.

**Creating highlights:** manually via the Highlight button or Ctrl+1, or automatically as the platform detects high-interaction moments. Manage everything afterward via the end-of-LIVE pop-up or through LIVE Center.

**Editing tools, from basic to advanced:**

1. **Basic editing (LIVE Center)** — trim clips, auto-generate editable captions (with adjustable length/content), add background music from the built-in library, add styled text/titles (with style suggestions based on your footage), and adjust canvas framing for mobile (split-screen, full-screen, etc.). Save progress as a draft anytime, then publish directly from LIVE Center when ready — posted videos land under **Posted** in Highlights and clips.

2. **AI smart editing (Autocut)** — one-click automated editing:
   - **Per-LIVE auto edit** — automatically selects your best highlights after each stream and builds a finished short with music and effects.
   - **Weekly recap** — stitches multiple highlights from the week into a narrative recap video.
   - Find finished Autocut videos at **LIVE Center > Home > Autocut videos** or **LIVE Content > Clips**.

3. **Secondary creation editing** — via the mobile app, you can authorize third parties to edit/post your replays or highlights (authorization managed in **LIVE Studio > Settings > Record** or on mobile).

4. **Advanced editing (CapCut)** — for anything beyond LIVE Center's quick-edit capability. With account linking, replays/highlights/edited videos sync directly to CapCut — no manual download needed.

**Sharing your best moments:**
- Download highlights and use dedicated editing software for music, captions, transitions, and post-dubbing.

## 11. Stream Relay Source — Embedding Licensed Livestreams

Stream relay lets you embed authorized third-party livestreams (like esports events) directly into your own LIVE, so you can react and commentate in real time — fully within TikTok's content-rights rules.

**Why use it instead of screen capture or a link source:** it pulls original-quality video straight from the official source, and because the content is pre-authorized, it eliminates the compliance risk that comes with relaying the same content another way.

**Setup:**
1. Get authorization from the content provider first.
2. **Add source > Stream relay**.
3. Choose **Featured events** (pick a live event during its window) or **Stream link** (paste the authorized link the provider gave you).

**FAQ highlights:**
- You can relay from multiple providers as long as each authorizes you separately.
- For a "clean" feed without official commentary, either use a clean-stream URL from the provider or relay from their alternate no-commentary TikTok account via Featured events.
- Authorizations don't expire on a fixed timer.
- If stream relay isn't showing up in your source list, you don't have authorization yet — go back to the content provider.
- Relaying the same content via capture sources or a link source instead of stream relay will likely violate Community Guidelines — only stream relay is compliant.
- You can switch providers mid-stream (with authorization) or drop back to your own content anytime by removing the source, then re-add it to resume.

## 13. Play Together — Turning Viewers Into Active Participants

Play Together lets you pull viewers directly into your gameplay — team battles, challenges, or open-world co-op — via a managed waitlist.

**What's new in the current version:** a bold in-LIVE board so viewers never miss the action, ready-made scenario templates (or build your own), and a smart waitlist sortable by wait time or contribution.

**Planning before you go live:**
1. Decide the format — team battles, viewer challenges, or casual world exploration.
2. Set rules in advance — win conditions, participation limits, special twists.
3. Launch it mid-stream without scrambling, because the groundwork's already done.

**Setting who can join the waitlist:**
- **All viewers** — widest reach.
- **Followers only** — keeps it exclusive to your core audience.
- **Creator's fans only** — for partnered/monetized-community collabs.

**Gameplay templates** — pick based on format: bring viewers into ranked/co-op matches via player ID for competitive team games; let them queue challenges (weapon picks, character picks, special rules) for challenge-driven formats; or open your room ID for free-roam/social games, mini-tournaments, or collaborative events.

**Connection method:**
- **Gather details when viewers apply** — best for games needing friend invites (e.g. MOBAs); viewers submit their info (like player ID) up front.
- **Send details to viewers you pick** — best for sandbox/co-op games; you share info like a room ID with whoever you select.
- Tip: turn on **Show details in LIVE** so all viewers (and waitlisted applicants) can see the gameplay description and their queue status.

**Picking participants:** sort the waitlist by wait time or contribution (or use **Random pick** for spontaneity), copy the picked applicant's details (revisit past picks under **All picks**), then connect in-game — picked viewers get notified and can join as instructed; shout them out by username to help them get ready.

**As a viewer:** tap the Play Together banner, meet the listed requirements, and join the waitlist. Once picked, you'll get a notification with instructions — but you need to stay in the LIVE while waiting, since leaving drops you from the queue.

## 15. Thanking Top Gifters Before Ending Stream

**Why this matters:** a genuine, structured thank-you at the end of a stream does four things — it honors supporters at their peak moment of recognition, builds a ritual that reinforces viewing habits, drives loyalty (recognized fans come back), and signals to everyone else that supporting you earns real respect.

**Interaction strategies:**

1. **Personalize it — don't sound like a bot.** Skip generic "thank you, [Name]" and reference something specific: advice they gave, a joke, a match they cheered you through. Example: *"Special thanks to our top gifter, [Name], who's been encouraging me through this whole game — this win belongs to both of us."*

2. **Thank everyone, not just the top 3.** A collective shout-out to everyone who stuck around until the end costs nothing and builds broader loyalty. Example: *"Shout out to everyone still hanging out here — your support and presence mean the world to me!"*

3. **Run a 3-step closing ritual:**
   - Recap the stream's best moment and credit it to fan support.
   - Do an official roll call — names 1st through 10th — pausing for the top 3 to share a specific compliment or memory.
   - End with a hook for tomorrow's stream.

**FAQ:**
- **Should you stay formal if the leaderboard is full of regulars?** Yes — long-time followers value the shoutout most and are the ones most likely to disengage if they feel taken for granted.
- **Will a long roll call bore other viewers?** Keep it to 1–3 minutes and bring real energy — don't read it flat like a checklist.

## 16. Viewer Wishes — Letting Viewers Vote With Gifts

Viewer Wishes lets viewers indicate a preference (a dance, a song, an in-game action) by sending a matching Gift. In gaming LIVEs, it can connect directly to in-game actions through Game Control or supported Interactive Games. Points update live so you can see what's winning and decide when to respond.

**Setup:** open **Viewer Wishes** from Tools (or from the game details screen in supported Interactive Games) before going live. Apply a preset template for faster setup, or build a fully custom version.

**Using a preset:** click **Select template** — for gaming, choose a Game Control template; some supported Interactive Games also offer game-specific templates.

**Custom wishes:** add 2–6 options, each pairing an action you'll take with a specific Gift.

**Before going live:** preview the Gift bar to confirm it doesn't cover your main scene.
- Gaming and landscape scenes only support the **vertical Gift bar** (collapsible, leaves more room for your main content).
- Supported portrait Group LIVE scenes can use a **horizontal Gift bar** across the top, useful for comparing points at a glance.

**Going live with it:** once live, confirm you've added an option for each of your 2–6 chosen Gifts, then click **Start now**.
