## 1. The Interaction Toolkit — Full Overview

TikTok's monetization tools fall into five groups. Data from TikTok shows 85% of creators who lean into these tools see measurable follower and reward growth — this isn't optional polish, it's core to the business.

**Direct viewers to support:**
- **Goal source** — displays a follower count, Gift value, or subscription count goal on screen.
- **LIVE goal** — set a target for specific Gifts (around 70% of creators who set one hit it).
- **Gift Gallery** — viewers "light up" a Gift by sending the required amount and become that Gift's title gifter.
- **Extendable countdown source** — viewers extend your LIVE by gifting or subscribing; built for marathon streams.

**Monitor key engagements:**
- **Alert source** — pop-up notifications for Gifts, new followers, subscriptions.
- **Viewer ranking source** — a leaderboard of top viewers as a form of appreciation.
- **Gift notifications** — a private voice alert (only you hear it) for every Gift received.

**Preparing for game kickoff** — tools to lock in commitment before you even start playing:
- **Guessing game** — viewers guess your in-game performance and earn points for correct guesses.
- **Poll & Gift vote** — engage viewers in decisions ("Which hero should I play?") via voting.
- **Play Together** — viewers apply to join activities or games with you directly (full detail in Module 6, Lesson 13).

**Rewarding your viewers** — creators who offer rewards see a meaningful bump in watch time per viewer:
- **Treasure Box** — host a Coin giveaway, with a customizable number of eligible collectors.
- **Goody Bag** — a bag of Coins viewers collect by completing a required interaction.
- **Game Rewards** — partner with third-party publishers to hand out free in-game items.
- **Game LIVE partnership** — partner with game publishers to recommend games with a download link.

**Spicing up game breaks** — creators who go live with others during downtime tend to collect more Diamonds in their next gaming session:
- **Co-host** — collaborate with up to 3 other creators to expand reach.
- **Multi-guest LIVE** — invite viewers to go live with you directly for a personal chat.

**Building your community:**
- **Fan Club** — your core gifter squad; fans level up by sending more Gifts.
- **Subscription** — a paid subscriber community with exclusive benefits and predictable monthly revenue.

## 2. Five Strategies for Earning Cash Rewards

1. **Build your team.** Moderators, co-hosts, and active community members help manage chat, promote streams, and keep the vibe positive — which directly translates to more monetization opportunity.
2. **Start a Fan Club.** Offer recurring supporters exclusive emotes, Gifts, and perks; Super Fans get faster leveling, an upgraded badge, and Popular LIVE bonuses. It builds steady income and a real sense of belonging for your most loyal viewers.
3. **Set a LIVE goal.** A visible target (follower count or Gift total) gives viewers a concrete reason to contribute — celebrate milestones as they hit to keep the momentum going.
4. **Run a Gift vote.** Let viewers vote on what happens next by sending Gifts — it's engagement and monetization in one move, and it teaches you what your audience actually wants.
5. **Start a LIVE match.** Friendly competition with another creator, powered by viewer Gifts — full mechanics in the next lesson.

## 3. Setting Up a LIVE Goal (formerly "Wishlist")

A LIVE goal displays your objective on screen to motivate contribution — this single feature has helped over 1M creators collect more Gifts.

**Why it works:** it gives viewers a structured, visible way to support you, and turns "watching" into "participating in something together."

**How to run one well:**
1. **Introduce the goal** — explain what happens when it's hit; a good pitch inspires action.
2. **Select and manage Gifts** — choose which Gifts count and set the target quantity (remember: Gifts cost Coins, so pick Gifts at a sensible value for your audience).
3. **Display the goal** — add a goal source so viewers are constantly reminded.
4. **Set contributor rewards** — offering something extra to the top 3 contributors (like playing together) drives more participation toward the goal.

**Setup steps:** click the LIVE Goal feature, open the pop-up, select your goal description, pick the corresponding Gift and confirm the quantity, then set any contributor prize and enable the sticker.

**Tips for setting a goal that actually works:**
- Know your own numbers — reward capacity, income level, how interactive your LIVE typically is — before setting a target.
- Balance difficulty: too easy doesn't inspire action, too hard feels pointless.
- Promise something specific for hitting it — a talent show bit, a heartfelt thank-you, whatever fits your content.

**After hitting the goal:** thank your audience and actually deliver the promised prize — following through is what makes the next goal work too.

## 4. Running a LIVE Match

A LIVE match is a friendly competition between co-hosting creators, scored by viewer support — a highly effective way to spike engagement and Gifts at once.

**How scoring works:** every coin sent = 1 match point, every like = 3 match points. Highest total at the end wins.

**Starting a match:** co-host with another creator, click **Start match**, and once they accept, it's on.

**Match variants:**
- **All-gift match** — every gift and like counts toward the score.
- **Specific-gift match** — only a chosen Gift type counts (e.g., a "Roses only" match) — viewers can still send other Gifts, they just won't score.

**During the match:**
- Runs for **5 minutes**.
- The top 3 contributors for each side get recognized during the victory lap, shown at the bottom of the screen.
- Leaving the match early automatically hands victory to your opponent — so only end deliberately.
- If a creator ends the match themselves within the 5 minutes, they're counted as the loser.

**After the match:**
- **Draw** — both creators return to the normal co-host scene.
- **Clear winner** — a 3-minute victory lap runs, where the winner can assign a task to the loser.

**Multi-host LIVE match:** up to 4 creators can compete as two teams. Click **Start match**, pick your teammate (the rest form the opposing team), and team points combine both members' totals. If a teammate wants to leave, you're notified and can click **Go back** to continue or **Leave** to exit — no response within 20 seconds and the match just continues automatically.

## 5. Gift Thanking — Acknowledging Support the Right Way

**What actually counts as Gift thanking:** a Gift (or an in-app support action like joining a Fan Club, becoming a Super Fan, or subscribing) → a clear response from you → and that response has to be connected to the Gift/support action specifically. A generic "welcome" or thanks for a follow/share doesn't count.

**Five patterns that work:**
1. **Thank by name** — "Thank you, Alex!"
2. **Thank the Gift** — "Thanks for the Rose!" (good when names are hard to read or things are moving fast)
3. **Thank both together** — "Thank you Alex for the Rose, that helps a lot."
4. **Thank Fan Club/Super Fan support** — "Thanks for joining the Fan Club!" (keep this specifically about TikTok LIVE in-app support — don't mix in Twitch/YouTube/Patreon/PayPal thanks)
5. **Thank the group during a rush** — "Thank you guys for all the Gifts!" when too many come in to name everyone individually.

**What does NOT count as Gift thanking** (and shouldn't be treated as if it does):
- "Thank you for the follow" — that's a follow, not a Gift.
- "Thanks for sharing the LIVE" — that's a share.
- "Welcome Alex to my LIVE" — that's a welcome, not an acknowledgment of support.
- Just saying "Alex" — reading a name isn't thanking.
- "Thanks for the Twitch subscription" — off-platform support isn't TikTok LIVE in-app support.

**The core principle:** keep it timely, specific, and clearly tied to the actual support action — that's what makes a viewer feel genuinely seen.

## 6. Ranking Push Guidance — Motivating a Leaderboard Climb

Ranking push guidance is asking viewers to help you move on a TikTok LIVE leaderboard. A valid push needs all three of: it's directed at viewers, it names a specific leaderboard goal (top 10, top 5, #1, holding rank, passing a specific creator), and it links a concrete viewer action to that goal.

**Five patterns:**
1. **Direct push** — "Help us push into the top 10." Clearest pattern: goal and action in one line.
2. **Rank defense** — "Keep tapping, don't let us drop from number one." For holding an existing position.
3. **Overtake/position contest** — "We're closing the gap, send Gifts now and let's pass them." Creates urgency.
4. **Semi-direct hinting** — "We're rank 11th, just a little more and we're on the board." Works only when context makes the ask obvious — otherwise, be more direct.
5. **Non-paid action push** — ranking push doesn't require Gifts; likes, shares, comments, staying, or joining the Fan Club all count if clearly tied to a leaderboard result.

**What does NOT count:**
- "We're number 8 right now" — that's just an announcement, no ask.
- "Tap the screen if you like the LIVE" — asks for engagement but doesn't tie it to a rank.
- "Thank you all for the Gifts" — that's Gift thanking, not a push.
- "Let's fill the Gift Gallery" — an internal goal, not a leaderboard rank.
- "Don't let them win this PK" — a match/PK goal, not a leaderboard rank.
- "Push push push!" — not enough unless the leaderboard goal was already established.

**The principle:** a good ranking push tells viewers three things at once — which leaderboard, what rank goal, and what to do right now.

## 7. Small Gift Guidance — Lowering the Barrier to a First Gift

Small Gift guidance is actively steering viewers toward a low-cost Gift (typically ≤5 Coins/Diamonds, like a Rose or a GG). It requires the creator to initiate it, name a small Gift specifically, and phrase it so the viewer instantly understands the action.

**Why it works:**
- **Lowers the support barrier** — makes new viewers feel like they can participate without a big spend.
- **Builds LIVE momentum** — a simple recurring prompt keeps energy up.
- **Creates repeatable engagement** — regulars learn the rhythm, newcomers can copy it.
- **Connects support to feedback** — tying the Gift to a shout-out or mini-response deepens the sense of participation.

**Five patterns:**
1. **Name the Gift directly** — "Drop a Rose if you're enjoying the vibe!"
2. **Make it feel light** — "Even a single Rose means a lot!"
3. **Repeat to build rhythm** — "Let's get those GGs rolling!"
4. **Tie it to a response** — "Drop a GG and you get to pick my next character."
5. **Pair voice with on-screen text** — e.g. "Rose 🌹 = Pick my next character" displayed on screen, which helps viewers who join mid-stream catch up instantly.

**Quick reference lines:**

| Goal | Line | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Ask for support | "Drop a small Gift to support me." | Casual/daily streams |
| Name a Gift | "Send me a Rose." | When you want total clarity |
| Lower pressure | "No need for big Gifts, a small one means a lot." | Streams with lots of new viewers |
| Build momentum | "Come on, GGs in the chat." | High-energy moments |
| Add engagement | "Send one and I'll keep going." | Challenge/performance/reaction content |

**FAQ:**
- **Do you always need to name the Gift?** Not required, but clarity always converts better.
- **Are longer lines more persuasive?** No — short, direct lines outperform in LIVE.
- **Can you thank and guide in the same breath?** Yes, as long as the small-Gift ask stays clear.
- **Does on-screen text help?** Yes, especially paired consistently with what you're saying out loud.
