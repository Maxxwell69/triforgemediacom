# Lesson: TikTok LIVE Gaming Club
### TriForge Media Creator Onboarding — Module 9

For gaming-focused TriForge talent, the Gaming Club is the structured path from "just streaming" to a supported, leveled creator career — policies, the Active Viewer metric, Creator Levels, content-quality rules, and the Gaming LIVE Pro program.

---

## 1. What the Gaming Club Actually Is

A single hub covering everything a LIVE gaming creator needs: program policies, content quality standards, the Active Viewer metric, Gaming LIVE Pro, and the Creator Levels system.

**Three pillars:**
- **Clear policies** — know exactly what counts toward programs and leaderboards.
- **Active Viewer focus** — understand the one metric that actually drives your standing.
- **Level-based support** — real perks that scale as you grow from Cadet to Legend.

Program policies prioritize authentic gameplay, on-camera presence, and meaningful chat interaction. Opportunities — showcases, badges, coaching — are largely level-based, so a higher Active Viewer average unlocks more support. All perks, placements, and opportunities are at TikTok's discretion and can change over time.

---

## 2. Active Viewer — The Metric That Actually Matters

**Definition:** the number of unique viewers who watched your LIVE for at least 3 *consecutive* minutes within a given date range — not just people who clicked in and immediately left.

**Why it's the metric to obsess over:**
- **Algorithm magnet** — streams that hold attention and drive chat send strong quality signals.
- **Real connection** — it isolates viewers who actually stick around and engage, not drive-by traffic.
- **Growth multiplier** — as Active Viewer climbs, followers, total viewers, and earnings tend to follow.

**Where to find it:**
1. Open the TikTok app → **LIVE Center**.
2. Tap **LIVE Analytics**.
3. Set the date range to **7 days**.
4. Tap **View all** under **Viewership**.
5. Find the metric labeled **Active Viewer**.

Note: the 7-day figure shown is a **total** — divide by 7 to get your daily average, which is what determines your Creator Level.

**Habits that grow Active Viewer:**
- Greet everyone by name, balancing attention between regulars and newcomers.
- Use simple prompts ("What should I play next?", "How's your day going?") to keep chat moving.
- Stream on a consistent, repeatable schedule.
- Run themed events — special nights, challenges, collabs — that give people a reason to stay longer.
- Thank every gifter with real energy so supporters feel seen and stick around.

## 3. Creator Levels — What You Unlock as You Grow

Levels are based on your **daily average Active Viewer over a 7-day period** (7-day total ÷ 7).

**Cadet Creators** — under 100 daily average Active Viewers
- Discord resources and guides
- Showcase opportunities as you grow
- Exclusive events and trainings
- Ticketed support system access

**Veteran Creators** — 100–500 daily average Active Viewers
- Spotlight and showcase opportunities
- Feature beta access
- Invite-only promos and events
- Workshops and creator training

**Champion Creators** — 500–2,000 daily average Active Viewers
- Gaming LIVE Pro badge eligibility
- Official account takeover opportunities
- 1:1 coaching-style sessions
- IRL and online event access

**Grandmaster Creators** — 2,000–5,000 daily average Active Viewers
- Custom show blueprints and formats
- Spotlight upgrades in campaigns
- Direct LIVE show feedback from the TikTok team
- Priority account support

**Legend Creators** — 5,000+ daily average Active Viewers
- Exclusive brand deals
- A dedicated support unit
- In-app promotion for key shows
- Flagship exposure across programs

**For TriForge:** this is a real ladder to track for every gaming creator on the roster — knowing where someone sits (and what the next tier unlocks) should directly inform coaching priorities.

## 4. Content Quality Guidelines — What Counts, What Doesn't

TikTok reviews which formats qualify for Gaming campaigns, leaderboards, and bonus evaluation. Repeated use of non-qualifying formats — or anything that manipulates watch time, gifting, or engagement metrics — can reduce visibility or program eligibility entirely.

**Qualifying content** centers on authentic, interactive, skill-driven gameplay that builds real creator-audience connection:
- Massive multiplayer/persistent-world games with long-term progression and shared storytelling.
- Social, co-op, and party games built around teamwork and humor.
- Strategy, card, puzzle, and management titles where you explain your logic and involve chat in decisions.
- Creative sandbox/building/simulation games where you co-create with your audience.
- Adventure, action, and story-driven games with real-time reaction and narrative investment.
- Competitive, skill-driven games showcasing mastery and clutch plays.

**Non-qualifying content:**
- Simplified/clone mini-games (basic snake, word puzzles, balloon pop) with no creative twist.
- Chance-based mechanics — random spins, cards, coins, slots — that rely on luck over skill.
- Marketplace/passive social streams centered on trading, selling, or idle chat with no real gameplay.
- Repetitive mini-game loops (constant swiping/tapping/matching) without evolving goals.
- Automated or idle play where the game mostly runs itself and you barely interact.

## 5. Gaming LIVE Pro

A program for gaming creators who consistently deliver high-quality streams, strong engagement, and adherence to the Content Quality Guidelines.

**Eligibility checklist — all of the following are typically required:**
- At least 50% of your LIVE hours in Gaming over the last 14 days.
- At least 50 valid LIVE hours in the last 30 days (~12.5 hours/week).
- At least 300,000 Diamonds earned in the last 30 days, or 100+ Super Fans.
- An average of 100+ concurrent viewers over the last 30 days.
- Consistent adherence to the Content Quality Guidelines.
- Champion, Grandmaster, or Legend role in the TikTok Gaming Club Discord.
- Participation in at least one Gaming campaign per month.
- Completed identity verification.

**What it can unlock:** better visibility in Gaming programs and premium showcases, deeper feedback and strategic guidance from TikTok's team, and opportunities to lead flagship shows, campaigns, or experiments. This program is built around long-term partnership with creators building sustainable channels, not one-off wins.
