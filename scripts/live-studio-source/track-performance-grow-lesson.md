# Lesson: Track Your Performance and Grow
### TriForge Media Creator Onboarding — Module 5

Content and setup only get a creator so far — the ones who actually grow are the ones who read their own data and adjust. This module covers where to find your metrics, what each one actually means, and how to use Creator Camp's challenges to level up.

---

## 1. Where to Find Your Metrics

There are two layers of data available to every creator: live, in-the-moment metrics, and a deeper analytics hub for reviewing past performance.

**During a LIVE:** real-time metrics on screen show you how the stream is performing as it happens — this is your in-the-moment read on viewer engagement.

**LIVE Center — your performance hub:** click the LIVE Center icon in the top-right corner of LIVE Studio to open it. It has four tabs:

- **Home** — your dashboard. Shows the last 7 days of LIVE duration, total views, Diamonds collected, likes, and new followers; lets you replay past streams in LIVE recordings; surfaces your top gifters, top viewers, and similar creators; and shows Trending Games on TikTok so you know what's pulling viewers right now.
- **Analytics** — the deep-dive tool covered in Lesson 2 below.
- **LIVE content** — replay, edit, and post your LIVE videos to TikTok to pull in new viewers and show off your best moments.
- **Creator Camp** — challenges and rewards for leveling up, covered in Lesson 3 below.

## 2. Reading Your Analytics — What Every Metric Actually Means

The **Analytics** tab in LIVE Center is where real growth decisions get made. It has two views: **Overview** and **Video analysis**.

**Overview tab** — pick any range from the last 7 to 60 days, and see:
- **Key metrics**, broken into four buckets: Viewership, Engagement, Rewards, Activity (full breakdown below).
- **Know your viewers** — audience demographics: traffic sources, country/region, age, gender. This is what should actually be driving your content decisions, not guesswork.

**Video analysis tab** — performance for each individual LIVE video, filterable by most recent, most Diamonds, or most viewed, so you can see exactly what's working.

**Viewership metrics:**
- **Views** — total times your LIVE videos were viewed.
- **Unique viewers** — individual people who watched.
- **Average watch time** — how long viewers stick around on average; the clearest read on whether your content is actually holding attention.
- **Peak concurrent viewers** — the highest simultaneous viewer count you hit.
- **Average concurrent viewers** — the average simultaneous count across the stream.

**Engagement metrics:**
- **New followers** — gained during that LIVE.
- **Gifters** — viewers who sent Gifts (virtual items ranging from simple images to animated effects, visible to everyone watching). Thanking gifters on stream tends to encourage more.
- **New subscribers** — viewers who subscribed during the LIVE, unlocking predictable monetization plus subscriber perks like badges, private chats, and subscriber-only LIVEs.
- **Viewers who commented** — unique commenters.
- **Comments** — total comment volume.
- **Likes** — total likes received.
- **Shares** — number of times the LIVE was shared.

**Rewards metrics:**
- **Diamonds** — collected from your content's popularity, redeemable for monetary rewards.

**Activity metrics:**
- **LIVE duration** — total time spent live.
- **Total LIVE videos** — how many times you've gone live.
- **Active days** — number of distinct days you streamed.

**The takeaway for TriForge talent:** don't just glance at views. Watch time, concurrent viewers, and the demographic breakdown are what actually tell you whether content is working and who it's working for.

## 3. Creator Camp — Structured Growth Through Challenges

Creator Camp gamifies improvement: complete challenges, collect rewards, and get recognized against other creators. Access it via **LIVE Center > Creator Camp** (note: availability is limited to certain creators, not guaranteed for everyone).

**Two challenge tracks:**

**Gaming level challenge** — compete against other gaming creators across 7 levels, reset monthly. Only metrics from gaming LIVEs in LIVE Studio count toward leveling. Each level has its own ranking (you'll see your position as "No. n"), and each level's detail page shows what's needed to advance, the rewards on offer, and any special features unlocked. Creators who climb past Level 6 get featured in the **Hall of Victors** — real recognition within the community.

**Reward challenge** — complete daily, weekly, and bonus tasks to earn gems, redeemable for flares, limited themes, subscription presents, and more.
- **Daily challenges** — refresh every day, short-term goals.
- **Weekly challenges** — refresh weekly, bigger objectives (click **Show tip** on any weekly challenge for advice on how to clear it).
- **Reward milestones** — track cumulative gems collected; hit a milestone, claim the matching reward.
- **Bonus reward** — hit a specific daily goal for a set number of days within a week to unlock a bonus (check it via the gift icon).

You can track progress in the top-right corner of LIVE Studio at any time, and you'll get notified on the end-LIVE page whenever you complete a challenge, unlock a reward, or level up.
