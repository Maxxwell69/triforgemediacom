Great visuals pull viewers in, but sound is what keeps them there. This module covers transforming your voice with audio effects and using sound cues to punch up the energy of a stream.

## 1. Transforming Your Voice With Audio Effects

Rich, well-produced sound is what separates a captivating stream from a flat one. LIVE Studio's audio mixer gives you three tools to shape your voice: templates, an equalizer, and reverb.

**Getting there:** open the **audio mixer** in the control bar, click your microphone's settings button, and select **Audio effects**.

**Templates** — one-click presets that instantly reshape your voice (younger, deeper, more energetic, more "special"). The fastest option when you want a change without manually tuning anything.

**Equalizer (EQ)** — for actual tonal control over your voice, adjusted across frequency bands:
- **Low frequencies (bass)** — boost for warmth/power, cut to reduce muddiness.
- **Mid frequencies** — control how present and clear your voice sits in the mix; push forward or pull back as needed.
- **High frequencies (treble)** — boost for brightness/airiness, cut to soften harshness.
- Use **Smooth curve** for gentler transitions between adjustments instead of abrupt changes, or click **Custom** to jump straight to presets.

**Reverb** — simulates ambiance, adding echo/depth as if you're in a hall, small room, or outdoors:
- **Early reflection** — the initial burst of sound reflections.
- **Decay time** — how long the reverb tail lasts.
- **Room size** — perceived size of the simulated space.
- **Stereo width** — spatial width of the effect.
- **Wet/Dry mix** — balance between processed and original signal.
- Click **Custom** here too for quick preset switching.

**Always test before going live:** click **Test audio** after setting up effects to hear exactly what your audience will hear, and adjust from there.

## 2. Playing Sound Effects to Boost the Atmosphere

Sound effects add interactivity and fun, giving your stream more texture and keeping the energy up at the right moments. Access them via the sound effects icon in the control bar.

**Built-in sound effects and when to use them:**

| Effect | Best for |
|---|---|
| Fail | Humorous or unexpected in-game defeat |
| Clap | Celebrating achievements or positive moments |
| Kiss | Showing appreciation or affection |
| Boing | Funny or surprising moments |
| Cheer | Applauding great plays or milestones |
| Laughter | Adding a comedic touch |
| Heartbeat | Tense or suspenseful moments |
| Wow | Impressive moments |
| Scream | Scary or dramatic scenes |
| Magic | Highlighting magical or surprising events |
| Triumph | Marking major in-game victories |
| Awkward | Embarrassing, laughable moments |
| Whistle | Grabbing viewers' attention |
| Drum | Dramatic buildup |
| Thriller | Suspenseful moments |

**Adding your own effects:** click the **+** icon in the sound effects panel and select local audio files to expand the library with sounds that fit your personal brand.

**Making effects faster to use mid-stream:**
- **Rearrange** — drag and drop to reorder the panel.
- **Pin to top** — right-click an effect and select **Pin** to keep your favorites within instant reach.
- **Hotkeys** — right-click an effect, choose **Hotkey**, and assign a key combo so you can fire it without touching the panel at all.
